Understand the signal rules
NO BLACK BOX

An experimental scalp setup.

A 5-minute trend filter with a 1-minute pullback—not a prediction.

CLOSED-CANDLE ANALYSIS
01 5M BIAS

Follow the trend

BUY requires 5m EMA 9 above EMA 21; SELL requires it below. Only complete 5m bars are used.

02 1M TRIGGER

Wait for a reclaim

The closed 1m price must cross back above EMA 9 for BUY, or below it for SELL. The 1m EMAs must align with the 5m trend.

03 CONFIRMATION

Check the RSI

1m RSI 14 must be 52–68 for BUY or 32–48 for SELL. Otherwise, the read is WAIT.

At most one decision per closed 1m bar: five opportunities in five minutes, often fewer or none. Cached polling can delay or miss timely entries. Stale or incomplete data forces WAIT.

Other chart timeframes use a different, simple EMA/RSI alignment rule (BUY: RSI 55–70; SELL: RSI 30–45). No backtest, stop-loss, profit target or performance guarantee is included.
